Our charters, in detail

Four ways to charter, all crewed and all under a written agreement.

Program 01 · Four hours to a full day

Day charters

Most first charters are a half day, and most people underestimate how much the cruising area matters. We ask what you actually want — a sandbar, snorkelling, a quiet sunset — and match a vessel whose draft and speed suit that, rather than whatever is free.

  • 4 to 8 hours
  • Up to vessel capacity
  • Quoted per charter
  • Written agreement

Program 02 · Several days, planned properly

Term charters

Term charters need real planning: fuel, provisioning, marina reservations and a route with alternatives when the forecast moves. We build the itinerary with the captain rather than selling you a brochure route the vessel cannot comfortably make.

  • 2+ days
  • Private parties
  • Quoted per charter
  • Advance booking

Program 03 · Ordered before you arrive

Provisioning & catering

Nobody wants to start a charter at a supermarket. Tell us what the group eats and drinks, including allergies and anyone not drinking, and it is aboard and stowed before you step on. Alcohol is provided in line with the charter agreement and Florida law.

  • Per charter
  • All charters
  • Quoted separately
  • Advance notice
